The insurance core: how the system is put together

Read from the code, manifests and templates on main, 15 Nov 2026. Every process and module drawn solid is built and tested on main. Dashed means designed but not built or not connected.

Nothing is deployed. The Azure templates (infra/*.bicep) and Kubernetes manifests (deploy/k8s) compile in CI, and the whole arrangement runs on one machine under Docker Compose, but no Azure subscription has been deployed to yet.

Front doors (stateless Deployments on AKS)

Portal service ic-portal

Every web page and app API (crates/channels). Maps a sign-in to a party ID, checks it with Cedar policies, forwards to the module or service that owns the work.

Core API ic-api

Checks the Entra token and role, finds the partition, proposes the command to its leader with the caller's idempotency key. Asks the people master before value leaves a policy.

Intake ic-underwriting-intake

Takes health disclosures and evidence into the clinical store, each person's record under a key of their own.

Reinsurer API ic-reinsurance

Statements and listings to reinsurers by client credentials, beside SFTP collection.

⇅Every process below names a person only by party ID (pty_…) and asks the people master for anything personal
People master: a separate system, not on the ledger (customer master, ic-customers, crates/customers)

Customer master ic-customers

Parties, relationships, contact points, vulnerability, representatives, due diligence, the person's own spending hold, retention and erasure. Its own PostgreSQL with per-party encryption keys wrapped in Key Vault, because personal data must be erasable and the ledger never forgets.

Asked by

group (/v1/parties/match), documents (contact-points), payments (spending-hold), core API (routing, authority), portal, new business (due-diligence).

Tells

Deaths to every partition (RecordDeath), holds (SetOwnerHold), owners and dependants to the channels module, changes on customer-events and customer-priority.

↓Commands: a named caller, a stable idempotency key, the workload's own cluster token↑Events: gapless per partition, no personal data
Ledger cluster: five voters per group: ic-node × 2 in Sydney, × 2 in Melbourne, and a witness in Canberra that holds no ledger. Raft (openraft) replicates every group; a majority must have an entry on disk before it is acknowledged.

64 partitions: the policy engine and the ledger

Each partition is its own Raft group, log, business date and double-entry hash-chained ledger: policies, products and rates as Starlark data, billing, arrears, lapses, loans, the investment ledger, cohort migration. The only writer of policies and money.

Claims engine

crates/claims inside each partition, so a decision and its postings commit together. Cross-partition offsets go as messages.

Read models

crates/readmodel: policy directory, life index, billing view, audience scope, inside replicated state.

Housekeeping by each leader

Seals journal segments to Blob, publishes events to Event Hubs, delivers cross-partition messages, hands leadership back after a region returns (fail-back).

⇅Drivers: stateless processes that carry a module's work to and from the outside world, each under its own managed identity
Drivers and the daily close (stateless Deployments and CronJobs on AKS)

ic-worker on Temporal

The end-of-day close (CronJob ic-close, 00:05 NZ) and unit orders (ic-unit-orders, every 15 min). Signs the day's Merkle root with Key Vault.

ic-payments-pump

Bank channels (ISO 20022 over mTLS), payment evidence, the weekly evacuation drill.

ic-documents

Rendering, the encrypted vault, email and SMS by Communication Services, print outbox.

ic-group

Employer files through a scanned quarantine, matching joiners with the people master.

ic-underwriting

Carries underwriting's effects to reinsurance, the core and group; daily sweep; notice hubs.

ic-reinsurance

Cessions to partitions, statements to write-once Blob and SFTP, exceptions to a hub.

ic-unit-pricing

Scanned valuation files in, prices to every partition, holdings extract out.

ic-finance-close

Valuation and experience extracts to write-once Blob, checked against signed roots.
