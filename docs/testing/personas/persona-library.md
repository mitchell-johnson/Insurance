# Persona Library

One hundred people, businesses and situations for testing the bank. Each persona is numbered so that scenario documents can refer to it. Part A is individuals (P1 to P60), Part B is businesses (B1 to B25), Part C is situations (S1 to S15). All names, places and details are invented.

## Part A: Individuals

### P1. Aroha Ngata, 19, Gisborne

First-year nursing student at EIT, living with her nan in Kaiti. Works 15 hours a week at a supermarket, paid fortnightly, and gets a student allowance. Opened her first account at 16 and still has it. Money is tight and runs out before payday most fortnights; she watches her balance several times a day. Everything is on her phone, an older Android with a cracked screen. Shares Netflix costs with three friends by bank transfer. ## Part C: Situations

Each situation names the personas involved and the sequence of events, so scenarios can be written against it.

### S1. The estate of Tom Clarke

Tom (husband of Vanessa, P57) dies suddenly at 49. Within a week: the bank is notified by Vanessa, then separately by a funeral director's letter; Tom's sole account and his business account must be restricted; the joint account must keep working for Vanessa; the home loan is in both names and repayments are due; life insurance will pay out in six weeks; direct debits in Tom's name keep arriving; an employer pays final wages into the sole account; the executor is Tom's brother, who lives in Perth. ### S2. Barry's romance scam

Barry (P50) has sent $8,000 and $15,000 to an overseas account over two months and is about to send $40,000. Monitoring flags the pattern. Staff must intervene: a warning, a hold on the payment, a conversation Barry resents, a scam report, an attempt to recall the earlier payments, and ongoing protection without removing his autonomy. His daughter calls the bank separately, worried, but has no authority. **Tests:** scam detection, payment hold and customer conversation, recall, third-party concern without authority, vulnerable customer flag, tipping-off boundaries do not apply but privacy does.

### S3. Dave's hardship

Dave (P13) misses two mortgage payments after redundancy. He does not contact the bank. Arrears buckets advance, letters go out, and a collections call finally reaches Kath. ### S4. Losa turns 18

### S5. The Patel skimming incident
