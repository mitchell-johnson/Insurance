# Operator op-e: SCN-16, SCN-17, SCN-18, SCN-22

Scripts and screenshots were kept outside the repository.
Everything I made starts with `scn16-`, `SCN17-`, `scn18-` or `scn22-`. No business day was closed.

**Shared state (SCN-18):** I used the **growth** fund. It is left as I found it in every way that matters: no suspension, no open price error (my error `err-7cb4c49c-…` is closed), and no price waiting. Prices for 2027-02-01 (v1) and 2027-02-02 (v4, the correction) are now in force. Prices cannot be removed, and the suite's `next()` simply carries on from 2027-02-03. One thing changed: the core's units on issue for growth were last recorded through 2027-02-01, and the suite records them again before each valuation. The SCN-16 self-grant I requested was declined by `admin`, so it is not active.

## Corrections to the scenario set-up (how it really works)
- SCN-16: `funding_forecast` needs `from` and `to` (dates). `control_totals` needs `run`. The MCP description of the query names neither `from` nor `to`. Payees were set up as `e2e/tests/06-payments.js` `payee()` does: `payee` enters, `payee2` confirms, and I played screening with `screen_payee`. The runs were made through the portal routes as `st10` (prepare_run, add_run_items, check_run), not on screen.
- SCN-18: the module prices in order from 2027-02-01. A fund's "next pricing date" is nine months before the business date, so "today's price" cannot be struck. Step 2 and step 5 are on the same date, as valuation revisions 1, 2 and 3. I raised the "day one" error on the latest price in force, as the suite does, because correcting an older price under a later one could leave the shared fund stuck. The customer page is `/customer`.
- SCN-22: `exposure` needs `on` and answers per reinsurer, not per life. `balance` needs `treaty` and `reinsurer`; `statements` needs `reinsurer`; `consent_status` needs `claim`. The treaty is `retail-life-surplus`; the reinsurers are `reinsurer-north` and `reinsurer-south`. No undeliverable-notice exception existed, so steps 3 and 4 used seeded notice 2 (a closed SCN-05 possible-match notice) through MCP `reinsurance_command`. My override also set the trauma sum to the life sum ($500k, $900k, $1.5m).
- Played parts: payee screening (SCN-16); cash matching receipt (SCN-17); unit pricing intake for valuations and core units (SCN-18); customer master record and sign-in binding (SCN-18, SCN-22).

## SCN-16 Treasury approver works the cut-off through an assistant: **completed**
I did all five steps as `sub-st17` over `/mcp`: tools/list, staff_me, the `me`, `open_runs`, `readiness`, `funding_forecast`, `control_totals` and `run` questions, approval of the clean run, approval of the blocked run, a repeat approval with a new key, a replay with the same key, the overreach list, an unknown run and a misspelt command. Then I checked `/payments` on screen.

What worked: the MCP approval is the same record as one made on screen (`/payments` shows "approved by st17"). A replay with the same key gives the same events and no second submission. Register, confirm and prepare are refused with the role named ("this needs the payee maintainer role" / "the maker role"). `claims_command` and `finance_command` are refused ("your roles do not include claims"). The self-grant waits for an administrator (`state: requested`), and approving her own grant is refused with `maker_checker`. On screen the blocked run shows "held: payee not confirmed" and offers no Approve.

Findings:
- **F-SCN16-1 (major), treasury approver and assistant.** The blocked run is refused without naming what blocks it. Steps: MCP `payments_command` `{"type":"approve_run","run":"scn16-run-blocked"}` as sub-st17. What happened: "Refused (409 items_held): held items are released or removed before approval". What should happen: name each held item, its payee, its amount in dollars and the reason (here `scn16-it-4` to `scn16-pay-u`, $999.00, payee not confirmed), and say who can clear it. Cause: `crates/payments/src/run.rs:615`.
- **F-SCN16-2 (major), approver.** A second approval is answered with a misleading reason. Steps: approve `scn16-run-clean` again with a new idempotency key. What happened: "409 wrong_state: a run is approved after it has been checked". The run had been checked, so this reads as if it had not. What should happen: "already approved by st17 at …; submission scn16-run-clean-1 is queued". Cause: `crates/payments/src/run.rs:606`.
- **F-SCN16-3 (major), assistant.** `control_totals` gives amounts in cents with no unit. Steps: `payments_question` `control_totals` `run=scn16-run-clean`. What happened: `"unsent": 165050`. The other answers at least say `total_minor` or `amount_minor`; this one has bare keys, so an assistant reads $165,050 instead of $1,650.50. The whole MCP payments surface answers in minor units, run types and statuses are codes (`lump_sum_claims`, `payee_not_confirmed`), and `staff_me` gives `recertification_due` as epoch seconds (1810441472) and limits in cents.
- **F-SCN16-4 (major), assistant.** Query fields are not described, and a misspelt command gets no list of commands. The `payments_question` schema lists only `account, exception, party, run, submission, case`, but `funding_forecast` needs `from` and `to`, and `deferred`, `fund_sweep`, `aged_unidentified_receipts`, `notices`, `incident` and others need fields that are not listed. `payments_command` with `type: "aprove_run"` or with no `type` answers "that is not a command people send to payments", with no valid names. Compare `staff_access`, which lists every variant. The `payments_command` schema gives only `{"type": "<command>", ...}`. Cause: `crates/channels/src/mcp.rs:358-362` and `crates/channels/src/http.rs:2543`. This confirms item 5 of "Things noticed".
- **F-SCN16-5 (major), treasury.** "Do we have the money?" cannot be answered. `funding_forecast` lists the outgoing runs (it totals both runs, $2,949.50, including the one that cannot be approved) and the claims waiting, but no fund balance or cash available. `readiness` answers "no drill has passed" for akl, mel and syd, with no words on whether today's file can go. (The drills may be a limit of the test stack.)
- **F-SCN16-6 (minor), approver.** The `find` refusal does not name the missing permission: "None of your roles allows this action. Ask your manager for the role or a one-off grant." It should say `customers.find`. The same text answers `staff_policy` for ri1. Cause: `crates/channels/src/staff.rs:315`.
- **F-SCN16-7 (minor), approver.** An approver can ask for a one-off `payments.maker` grant for herself. It is not refused at request time; it waits for an administrator, who could approve it and break maker/approver separation by a grant. (The per-run maker-is-not-approver check would still apply, but the request should be refused or flagged when it is made.) A requester also cannot withdraw her own request: `withdraw_grant` does not exist and `revoke_grant` answers "no such active grant". I had `admin` decline it.
- **F-SCN16-8 (minor), approver.** A question about a run that does not exist answers "404 not_found: no such resource". The command path says "there is no run scn16-nope". Cause: `crates/channels/src/http.rs:206`.
- **F-SCN16-9 (minor), staff using MCP.** The tool list is far larger than the person's job. st17 (and ri1) are offered 116 tools: every customer tool (`my_account` works and returns `party: st17, staff: true`), adviser and employer tools, and the customer-master tools nobody holds (`find_customers`, `merge_customers`, `customer_export`…). The guidance said 70 staff tools; it is 116. Every call is still checked by its route, and every overreach I tried was refused, but (b) of the MCP rule does not hold. Cause: `crates/channels/src/mcp.rs:482-499` (the customer realm is granted to any development sign-in). This confirms items 3 and 4.
- **F-SCN16-10 (minor), screen user.** `/payments` amounts have no currency (1299.00, 1650.50), payees show as references (`Payee: scn16-pay-a`), and "A run" still asks for a typed run ID (the open-runs rows do offer Open).

## SCN-17 Finance gets the month ready to close: **partly (mostly blocked by the stack)**
The ledger has received no business day ("books fed to no date yet"), because the end-of-day close workflow is not running and operators may not close days. Every write (journal, check report, waiver, both sign-offs, close) is refused "2027-11 has not started". This is a limit of the test stack, not a product defect. Even so, I did each step on screen as st10, st11, st12 and the auditor, and recorded what the screens do before the server answers.

What worked: the close lists every check with owner, due date and why it is red, in words. The waiver's check picker offers the red checks with their names. The auditor sees no enabled action button. The trial balance of an empty ledger reads "Nothing to show".

Findings:
- **F-SCN17-1 (major), finance operator.** A mistyped amount is not caught. It is silently cut up and sent. Steps: `/finance` as sub-st10, Journals tab, lines `6500, 1,2500.00, SF1-life` and `2500, -12500.00, SF1-life`, then Propose. What happened: no page error, and a POST was sent. The page splits on commas, so line 1 became account 6500, amount **$1.00**, fund **"2500.00"**, and `SF1-life` was dropped. It was refused only because the period had not started. What should happen: refuse a row with more than three fields, or a fund that is not a statutory fund, naming the row. Also check that debits equal credits on the page before sending. Cause: `crates/channels/ui/finance.html:154-156`.
- **F-SCN17-2 (major, read in the code; not reachable at run time), finance operator.** An unbalanced journal would be refused in millionths of a cent, not dollars. Being off by one cent would read "the journal is off by 1000000 in NZD". Cause: `crates/finance/src/books.rs:2007`; lines are `signed_fine`, and `FINE_PER_MINOR` is 1,000,000 (`crates/money/src/lib.rs:22`). It should read "debits are $0.01 more than credits".
- **F-SCN17-3 (major), whole finance team.** The dates filled in come from the browser's clock, not the business date, and they contradict each other. Steps: open `/finance` as st10. What happened: the close period defaulted to 2026-10. In Journals, the period was 2026-10 but the value date was 2026-11-17 (outside that period). The waiver remediation date was 2026-12-01, aged suspense "as at" was 2026-11-17, and "17 day(s) since the period ended". The core's business date is 2027-11-15 and the console header shows nothing of it. With no fed day, `likely()` falls back to `portal.month(1)` from `new Date()`. Cause: `crates/channels/ui/finance.html:138, 271` and `crates/channels/ui/app.js:340-342`. In production the clocks agree, so the stack makes this worse, but the value date and period still disagree on any day after the 1st. The same pattern appears on `/pricing` (F-SCN18-6) and `/reinsurance` (month-end check defaults to 2026-10-31).
- **F-SCN17-4 (major; may be a limit of the stack), finance operator.** The receipt with a mistyped policy number cannot be found anywhere. Set-up: as cash matching, I posted `POST /v1/payments` for $234.56 with reference `SCN17-POL-TYPO-9X`, which answered `202 exception: no policy matches this payment`. Then: finance Aged items as at 2027-11-15 shows "None, NZ$0.00". Payments `aged_unidentified_receipts` gives `items: []`, and `open_exceptions` gives `[]`. The documentation form (`#d-party`, `#d-since`) needs a typed party and date, and nothing offers this receipt. If the suspense report only arrives with the end-of-day close, the screen should say "no suspense report since …" rather than "None / NZ$0.00", which reads as nothing outstanding.
- **F-SCN17-5 (minor), finance lead.** On a period that has not started, sign-off and close are refused with only "2027-11 has not started". They should also list the red blocking checks and the sign-offs still needed (the summary on the page does list these).
- **F-SCN17-6 (minor), actuary.** "Sign off as appointed actuary" is enabled for November, which is not a quarter end, although "Still needed" names only the finance lead.
- **F-SCN17-7 (minor; it may be the feed), finance.** The conduct figures for 2027-11 show 0 accepted, while Accounting groups lists 10 policies issued this month. If the figures come only from fed days, the screen should say so. Compare suite E2E-159, which expects accepted ≥ 1.
- **F-SCN17-8 (minor), actuary.** The frozen extract is refused as "partition 0 has not closed 2027-11-30". "Partition" is an internal word to a finance user; the failed GET also shows as a console error.
- The account code is still typed (a known gap). I could not see whether a wrong code is refused by name, because the period is checked first. The code reads "the chart has no account {code}" (`crates/finance/src/books.rs:1990`), which is good.

## SCN-18 A unit price that moved 9% on a quiet day: **completed (adviser view not done)**
Growth fund, all on screens: st8 tried to prepare before any valuation, then before the 2027-02-01 valuation (refused, named). Day A was prepared by st8 and approved by st9 from the work list. Mid, buy and sell (1.0005 / 1.0031 / 0.9979) equal `oracle.prices`. For 2027-02-02, a file with units 5 units off came out **blocked**. A corrected file with a 9% move came out **held**. The maker was offered only Details. st9 read Details (each check with numbers) and rejected it with a reason. A third file with a 0.8% move was approved: 1.0085 / 1.0111 / 1.0059, equal to the oracle, and applied on partitions 0-3. st9l raised a price error. The next date (2027-02-03) was prepared and its approval refused while the error was open. A restated file was prepared as the correction and approved (v4, "immaterial"). The 02-03 price was refused as stale and rejected. st9l closed the error with a summary. A customer with an `invest_plan` policy opened `/customer`.

Findings:
- **F-SCN18-1 (major), pricing lead.** "It is wrong" on a price's row does nothing and throws a script error. Steps: `/pricing` as sub-st9l, Prices tab, fund growth, 2027-02-01 to 2027-02-05, Show prices in force, then "It is wrong" on `growth:2027-02-02.3`. What happened: page error `wrong.add is not a function`, and the Price errors form stays empty. The lead must instead pick the price in the "Wrong prices" field (the picker works after a lookup). Cause: `crates/channels/ui/pricing.html:37` calls `wrong.add(id)`, but `portal.pickMany` returns only `{values, clear}` (`crates/channels/ui/app.js:335`).
- **F-SCN18-2 (major), pricing maker.** A blocked price is announced as "Prepared growth:2027-02-02.1: blocked. Someone else approves it." A blocked price can be approved by nobody. The message should say it cannot be approved until the units agree. Cause: `crates/channels/ui/pricing.html:93`.
- **F-SCN18-3 (major), maker and checker.** Check details are given in micro-units and raw fractions. "units on issue: core 1000000000000 micro-units, administrator 1000005000000, difference 5000000" means 1,000,000 against 1,000,005 units, a difference of 5 units. "moved 0.089955 against a tolerance of 0.05" means 9.0% against 5%. Elsewhere: "net assets 1090545.00 against 1090500.000000 expected", an error size of "0.00069458", and "Units micro 0" under remediation. The valuation shows raw field names ("Nav", "Units on issue micro", "Restated: no", "Received at 2026-11-17T04:32:14Z"). Cause: the strike-check detail strings in `crates/pricing/src` (strike/state), shown as given by `crates/channels/ui/pricing.html`.
- **F-SCN18-4 (major), customer.** The customer cannot see their units or their value at all. Steps: `/customer` as the SCN-18 customer (Cbgfplf Kbkfpfnnoba, born 1993-02-14, policy SCN18-POL-IP1). What happened: the policy shows product `invest-plan` (a code), and Cover shows "account / life-1 / NZ$0.00". There is no section for funds, units, unit price or price date. `customer.html` has no holdings view at all; the adviser page does (`crates/channels/ui/adviser.html:36-43`). The policy holds no units yet (no premium, no orders), so the adviser view was not exercised. A customer appointing an adviser needs adviser and engagement references typed on the page ("Adviser reference", "Their engagement reference"). This confirms item 7 (generic products).
- **F-SCN18-5 (minor), checker.** While an error is open, the work list still offers Approve on the next date with no warning. The refusal then names the error by its UUID: "growth is held by price error err-7cb4c49c-0601-4373-b1a5-3466a16ac915 until it is corrected". It should say "held by the price error raised on 2027-02-04 against 2 Feb's price; approve its correction first". The correction row in Work waiting does not say which price it corrects.
- **F-SCN18-6 (minor), maker.** The pricing date defaults to the browser's today (2026-11-17), not the fund's next pricing date as the module reports it (2027-02-01, and it can only be that date). "Prices overdue today" is empty although growth has had no price for nine months of the business calendar. The fund picker shows codes (`balanced`, `cash`, `growth`), not names ("Growth Fund"). "Detected on" and the lookup range also default from the browser's clock.
- **F-SCN18-7 (minor), maker.** A reference is still typed: the "Wrong prices" field takes a price ID, but the picker offers only prices already looked up on the Prices tab ("Look up the fund's prices under Prices first").
- Confirmed as designed: the maker is offered only Details on her own price, a held price offers "Accept checks and approve", a blocked one offers no approval, and approving a price struck from a since-corrected previous price is refused as stale.

## SCN-22 Reinsurance reviews a large life and the month-end sweep: **partly (stack: no reinsurance driver, so no cessions)**
One life (Clkdbcg Knpoinkpcaj, born 1961-05-18, `pty_2609b73b…`) was issued on SCN22-POL-TL1, TL2 and TL3: life and trauma at $500k, $900k and $1.5m. ri1 opened `/reinsurance` (role and date shown correctly) and asked MCP `me`, `exposure`, `balance`, `statements`, `consent_status`, `notices`, `exceptions` and `sweep_check`. ri1 retried, then prepared two abandonments of notice 2. ri1 could not sign his own (refused with `separation`). The auditor could not sign (needs `reinsurance.approve`), act, or withdraw. ri1 withdrew the duplicate on screen. ri2 signed the other, and the module refused it ("notice 2 has not been refused"). Month-end check on screen and by MCP; finance close for 2027-10 and 2027-11 as st11; auditor on screen and MCP.

Findings:
- **F-SCN22-1 (major), reinsurance operations.** There is no way to see a life's accumulation, by screen or by MCP. The module has `Query::Retained { life, on }` and `Query::Cession { key }` (`crates/reinsurance/src/host.rs`, `views.rs:105`), but the console asks only per-reinsurer `exposure`. Cause: `crates/channels/src/reinsurance.rs:83-96`. ri1 cannot even get to the life: `find` and `staff_policy` are refused for ri-ops. So no, a person cannot reach a life's exposure, with or without typing an internal reference. With the driver not running, `exposure` is `{}` and `balance` is `0`, so retained against ceded, and whether $5.8m on one life (above the $4m automatic limit) is flagged for facultative referral, cannot be checked. No exception about it was raised. (The stack cannot cede without core events, which the driver delivers.)
- **F-SCN22-2 (major), finance lead.** The finance close does not see the reinsurance blocker. Reinsurance's sweep check for 2027-10-31 is red ("instructions not delivered; Count: 69"), but the finance close for 2027-10 shows `reinsurance_sweep` as "not due", with a due date of 2027-11-01, which is already past on the business date.
- **F-SCN22-3 (minor), reinsurance operations.** An abandonment can be prepared for a notice that cannot be abandoned, and the same one twice. `retry_notice` seq 2 answered "notice 2 has not been refused". Two `abandon_notice` seq 2 were then both accepted as "prepared", with no duplicate warning. The second person learns that it was pointless only when signing. After the refused signature the item vanishes, ri2's screen still lists it with "Sign and send" until a reload, and ri1 is told nothing.
- **F-SCN22-4 (minor), reinsurance operations.** Withdraw on the "Waiting for a second person" row works but shows no message.
- **F-SCN22-5 (minor), reinsurance operations.** Exceptions are not worked from their row. The `claim_fact_not_taken` rows have an empty action cell (`actionsFor` default `[]`, `crates/channels/ui/reinsurance.html:72`), although the fix ("Enter a claim fact by hand") is on another tab. The details show internals ("event 1-137", "unknown_claim:"). The facultative form wants a typed "Capacity reservation" reference. The month-end date defaults to 2026-10-31 (browser clock). The MCP query description names only `period_end, number, seq`, but `exposure` needs `on`, `balance` needs `treaty` and `reinsurer`, `statements` needs `reinsurer`, and `consent_status` needs `claim`, while treaty and reinsurer IDs appear nowhere in the answers.
- What worked: "not delivered" is said plainly with a count, on screen and by MCP, and agrees between them. The preparer can only withdraw. The auditor's refusals give the reason. The auditor is offered only Show and Check buttons.
