# Operator op-d: SCN-11, SCN-12, SCN-24, SCN-15

Scripts and screenshots were kept outside the repository.

Set-up I did off-screen (things a user could not do): customers made in the customer master (`customer()`); policies issued on the API (`POST /v1/policies`, as Journey.policy does); the SCN-24 claim notified and assigned through the portal route (claims part only); three SCN-15 payees registered and confirmed through the portal route (two maintainers, as `payee()` in 06-payments does); every screening result (`screen_payee`) and every bank message (begin_submission, submitted, acknowledgement with one rejection, settlement, a return, a statement) sent with System.tell as the bank driver and screening service. People's decisions all went through the screens.

Corrections to the scenario set-up:
- A health claim notified with plain "notify" (from a call) has no invoice amount, and no later command can add one. The only way to give a health claim its invoice is the separate action "notify a health invoice", which makes a **new** claim. So SCN-11 needs the invoice at notification time.
- `paybad` cannot confirm a payee at all (`role_conflict`: "an approver does not maintain payees or bank connectivity"). So SCN-15 step 4 ("a payee he confirmed") cannot be set up. The conflict is stopped earlier than the scenario expected.
- The scenario's funeral advance (SCN-12) cannot be done with the seeded rules (see F-12-1).
- A non-disclosure review on an in-force policy cannot be started anywhere (see F-24-1).

## SCN-11 A health claim with a missing invoice, above the assessor's limit — partly

What I did: I made customer "Cggaadg Kncaneepkaf" (born 1999-02-10) with health_family policy SCN11-POL-6WEDK-2. As st3, I used "Notify a new claim" from a call (CLM-20261117-3LA2N), took it from the queue, recorded the cover check and cause, and asked for the invoice and the surgeon's report. When the invoice "arrived" I recorded it (cost typed as 78.50) and tried to admit; the claim stayed stuck. As st3, I then used "notify a health invoice": first 78.50 with category "surgery" (CLM-20261117-WEY8E, later withdrawn), then $7,850 surgical (CLM-20261117-UDSC6). st3 was refused when she tried to take it. st4 took it from the health queue and admitted it: $7,350 after the excess, payable $7,125.75. The payee maintainer registered "Mercy Hospital" as a provider payee, payee2 confirmed it, and I played the screening. st4 "verified" the payee on the claim and paid. The payment showed in Payments waiting. The customer page shows the claim's status and no cause. I withdrew the two dead claims (3LA2N, WEY8E). No adviser or employer is on this policy, so I did not check those views.

Findings:

- **F-11-1 blocker. Claims payee verification is the assessor's own say-so and is not linked to the payments payee register.** It hurts the insurer (fraud control) and auditors. Steps: /claims as sub-st4, an admitted claim, action "verify payee", type anything in "Account reference", "Name check reference" and "Screening reference" (for example `match`, `clear`), then Send, then Pay. The claim is released for payment to whatever party was typed. What should happen: the claim picks a payments payee that two maintainers have confirmed and that has been screened, and the payment follows it. Cause: crates/claims/src/book.rs:442-445 checks only that the strings are not empty, and claims.html:84 types all four references. Payments then puts the waiting item on the party ("Payee: scn11-hospital-mercy"), not on the confirmed payee. The refusal before verification ("verify the payee before the first payment", book.rs:495) does not say who verifies payees or where.
- **F-11-2 major. A health invoice cannot be added to a claim notified by phone, and the screen still offers "Decide: admit".** It hurts the assessor and the customer. Steps: /claims as sub-st3, Notify a new claim (claim type health, cover hospital), take it, cover check, cause, ask for evidence "invoice", record it received. "Decide: admit" (blank, or 7350) is refused with "the amount is more than the cover pays for this event". Nothing on the claim can take the invoice amount; "Cost to pay the provider" on the evidence is a different thing. What should happen: an invoice can be recorded on an open health claim, or the phone notification asks for one. Cause: there is no command for it (crates/claims/src/command.rs; the invoice exists only on Notify, intake.rs:225). Also, the Claim type field defaults to "death" even after a hospital cover is picked.
- **F-11-3 major. An invoice amount typed wrong cannot be corrected; the only way out is to withdraw the claim and notify again.** Steps: notify a health invoice with "Invoice amount" 78.50. The claim opens reserved at 78.50, and no action changes it. I withdrew it as R-WDRAW. The free-text "Benefit category" took "surgery", which is not a category on the plan ("surgical" is), and it was accepted without a word. It should be a list from the plan. claims.html:40.
- **F-11-4 major. The cover check gives no facts.** It hurts the assessor. Steps: /claims as sub-st3, open the claim, action "cover check". The form has one free-text box, "Facts relied on (comma separated)", and the assessor has to type codes such as `in_force, definition`. The claim page shows no in-force status, premium paid to, definitions, exclusions, or surgical limits ($50,000 per event, $500 excess). The cover picker shows "hospital · NZ$0.00" three times, without the life's name. What should happen: the facts are shown, and the assessor ticks the ones she relied on. claims.html:45 and 120.
- **F-11-5 major. The over-limit refusal does not name the limit or who can take the claim, and the next steps offer what she cannot do.** Steps: CLM-20261117-UDSC6 (reserve 7850.00) as sub-st3, press "Assign it to me". She gets "st3 has no authority for a claim of this type and amount" (crates/claims/src/intake.rs:397). It should say "above your $5,000.00 limit; a senior assessor such as st4 or st4b can take it". She then presses "Assign to someone else" (also offered) and is refused with "only a queue lead or claims manager assigns a claim to someone else". So she has no way to hand it on; the claim just sits unassigned in the health queue. The colleague picker also lists st13 (an investigator) and n7, without saying who has the authority for this claim.
- **F-11-6 major. The claim shows "paid" and closes the moment it is released to payments, before any money moves.** It hurts the customer (who reads "paid") and the assessor. Steps: st4 pays CLM-20261117-UDSC6. The title reads "health, paid", Closed 2027-11-15, and /customer shows "paid" while the item sits in Payments waiting. Later the bank returned it (SCN-15), and the claim still reads paid, with the Returned column empty (see F-15-3).
- **F-11-7 minor. The payable amount is reduced with no explanation.** Steps: admit UDSC6. Admitted 7350.00, Payable 7125.75. The $224.25 is the unpaid first premium, but the screen does not say so, and the hospital is paid less than invoice minus excess. Meanwhile /customer shows Payments "paid" and "Nothing is overdue". One of the two is wrong.
- **F-11-8 minor. Searching by family name alone finds nobody, and nothing says why.** Steps: Notify a new claim, Policy field, type the family name "Kncaneepkaf". It says "Nothing matches that." The full name, or the family name plus birth date, does find her. `/staff/v1/find?q=Kncaneepkaf` gives people [] and hint null. It should find her or say "add the given name or birth date".
- **F-11-9 minor. Codes and internal references show where words should.** The cause code is typed (`CC-0204`), and the refusal "that cause code is not on the controlled list" does not list the codes. Reason codes are typed. "Life" offers life-1/life-2/life-3 instead of names. "Document in the vault" is a typed reference. After a policy is picked, the field shows `scn11-pol-6wedk-2` instead of the number. The claim summary shows the policy reference, not the number. Amounts are shown without a $ sign (7850.00), and the queue's "Priority" is a bare number. The queue rows have no customer name or policy number. Evidence "Kind" and "From" are free text, and the cost field is required even when nothing is owed (I had to type 0 on the death certificate).
- **F-11-10 minor. After "notify a health invoice" the new claim is not opened, and pressing Send again gives "claim … is already registered".** claims.html:166 opens the claim only when `action === "notify"`.
- Good: the queue loads by itself; outstanding evidence shows with a due date (2027-12-05); the excess is applied; /customer shows status and never the cause.

## SCN-12 A death claim: two signatures, a funeral advance and an estate account — partly

What I did: I made "Cdfjafn Klaeblaimnp" (born 1986-01-15), who died, with term_life policy SCN12-POL-78MU3-3, and the widow "Caacdeh Kampbnngpao". st4 found the policy by family name and birth date, notified a death claim (CLM-20261117-2M6Y3) with "Notified by: beneficiary" and the widow as claimant, and asked for the death certificate. The funeral advance was impossible (F-12-1). On the certificate, st4 recorded the cause and cover check and admitted. Her own countersignature was refused; st4b countersigned, and the claim was admitted at $500,000, payable $499,883.13. The maintainers registered and confirmed the estate payee, and I played the screening. The estate payee was blocked "needs legal approval" until `legal` approved it; the claims side had already paid by then. The policy shows as terminated with nothing outstanding. n7: I issued SCN12-POL-78MU3-4 to cust-p60 and raised SCN12-CLM-REL-BU3H on it.

Findings:

- **F-12-1 blocker (configuration). A funeral advance cannot be made: no seeded reason code is allowed for it.** It hurts the family and the assessor. Steps: /claims as sub-st4, an open death claim she owns, action "decide: funeral advance", Advance 20000.00 with reason R-OK. She gets "R-OK is not a reason code for this decision". R-EVID gives the same, and a blank reason gives "a decision needs at least one reason code". Neither the refusal nor the field lists the allowed codes. So the $15,000 cap could not be tested on screen. Cause: e2e/stack/seed.js `reason_codes` gives no code for `funeral_advance`. Also, the over-cap message in the code, "the advance is more than the rules allow before a decision" (crates/claims/src/decide.rs:478), does not name $15,000.00.
- **F-12-2 major. An assessor can see that a related party has a claim, and its status.** It hurts the related customer's privacy (US-N07-1). Steps: /claims as sub-n7, Claim tab, set a purpose, type "SCN12-POL-78MU3" in the claim field. The picker lists `SCN12-CLM-REL-BU3H · SCN12-POL-78MU3-4 · open`, the claim of cust-p60, her related party. Opening it is refused (404), but its existence, reference and status show. `GET /staff/v1/claims/policy?policy=scn12-pol-78mu3-4` as n7 returns it (crates/channels/src/claims.rs:225, Question::PolicyStatus, is not filtered by related_parties). The staff policy view `/staff/v1/policies/scn12-pol-78mu3-4` also shows n7 cust-p60's covers and billing, because no personal connection is declared for n7 in entitlements. The seed knows about cust-p60 only in the claims authority book.
- **F-12-3 major. Payments releases the claim to an estate payee before legal has approved it, and legal has no worklist.** Steps: st4 verifies payee kind estate with an "Authority document" reference and pays. The claim says "paid" and the item is waiting. In /payments as sub-legal, "Notices for your roles" says "Nothing is waiting", and legal could reach the payee only by typing the deceased's party reference into "A customer or payee party". The block does show in the payee row: "payee needs legal approval". It should show on legal's screen, and the claim should say it waits for legal.
- **F-12-4 major (configuration or product). The $499,883.13 estate payment is above the route's $100,000 item limit, and nothing says so.** Steps: st10 takes PAY-20261117-JBVMM into a lump-sum run and presses Check. It is held only as "payee cooling off". The item limit hold (crates/payments/src/run.rs:343) is checked only when no other hold applies, so the person sees one reason at a time. The claims side lets st4 release one item of $499,883.13. There is no honest path: claims cannot split the payment, and payments can only hold it. This confirms "Things noticed" item 6.
- **F-12-5 minor. The caller's relationship cannot be recorded.** "Notified by" has only policyholder, life insured, beneficiary, executor, employer, adviser, provider. There is no spouse or family member, and the caller's name is not taken. The claimant is picked by name, but the field then shows `pty_67e7…`, and the claimant appears nowhere on the claim page.
- **F-12-6 minor. "Signed: nobody yet. Still needed: 1." is shown while the proposer's own signature already counts.** The self-countersign refusal is good: "each signature must come from a different person".
- Not reachable: late interest (the claim is within 30 days); reinsurance recovery (SCN-22 owns it).
- Good: search by family name and birth date in NZ order works; the admission waits for a second senior; the policy ends with nothing outstanding; the estate payee is blocked until legal approves and the payee row says so.

## SCN-24 A claim that turns up non-disclosure — blocked (underwriting side)

What I did: I made "Ccnohoo Khejffmiajn" (born 1973-06-11) with income_protection policy SCN24-POL-7JYTW-2 and claim SCN24-CLM-7jytw-3, owned by st4. st4 asked for the GP notes and recorded them received. She looked for a way to refer the claim to underwriting and found none. As st2 I opened /underwriting; as st1 I looked at it too. st4 tried to decline with R-NDISC, was stopped until a claimant conversation was recorded, recorded one as a task, and declined; st4b countersigned. Then I opened /customer.

Findings:

- **F-24-1 blocker. Underwriting cannot start a non-disclosure review on an in-force policy, and a remedy cannot be an exclusion.** It hurts the senior underwriter and the claimant. Steps: /underwriting as sub-st2, "Remedies and review". The Review picker says "No non-disclosure review is in the queues you have open", and nothing starts one for a policy. The outcomes are only No action, Vary the terms (Loading %), and Avoid the policy; "an exclusion from inception" is not offered and there is no exclusion field. The reason is typed ("Reason codes"), not picked from the library. Cause: underwriting.html:44-52 and 203; reviews come only from intake queues, which are empty because the intake is not in the stack (test-stack limit). The missing "start a review for this policy" and the missing exclusion outcome are product gaps.
- **F-24-2 major. Claims has no way to refer a claim to underwriting.** Steps: /claims as sub-st4, the claim with GP notes received. The next steps and the action list offer nothing like "refer to underwriting"; the only related action is "close investigation". The assessor has to go outside the system.
- **F-24-3 major. A claim can be declined for non-disclosure with no underwriting remedy in place.** Steps: on SCN24-CLM-7jytw-3, cause and cover check recorded, then "decide: decline" with R-NDISC, a claimant conversation task completed, st4b approves. The claim is declined while the policy terms are unchanged. A non-disclosure decline should need a signed remedy.
- **F-24-4 minor. Missing disclosures show as an error, not "nothing on file".** Steps: /underwriting as sub-st2, "Read a life's disclosures", proposal = policy ref, life-1, with a purpose. The page shows "the underwriting intake is not configured here" (HTTP 503 `underwriting_intake_unavailable`) and logs a console error. This partly confirms the scenario's complication. The words say what is wrong but not what to do next. The proposal and life are typed.
- **F-24-5 minor. The customer sees "declined" with nothing else.** /customer shows `SCN24-CLM-7jytw-3 declined`, with no reason, no "what you can do", and no way to complain. It does not say "non-disclosure", which is correct.
- Good: st1 is not shown the remedy forms; st2 is told a second senior signs; the decline is refused until a conversation is recorded ("talk to the claimant before declining, and record it as a task"). Weakness: the task's "Attendees" are typed staff references; the claimant cannot be picked.

## SCN-15 A claims payment run where the bank sends one item back — partly

What I did: the SCN-11 and SCN-12 payments were waiting. I had three payees confirmed and screened (scn15-pye-a/b/c-cumy) and one unconfirmed (scn15-pye-bad-cumy). As st10 I prepared run RUN-20261117-N3YSU (lump sum claims), took both waiting payments, and added a/b/c, the unconfirmed one, and a second $1,200 to payee a. I pressed Check. As st17 I tried to approve and was refused. As st10 I tried to release the duplicate, removed the unconfirmed item, and removed the estate item, which then returned to waiting. paybad was refused. The only working way to release the duplicate hold was st17's "Another command" JSON box; even then both twins were held, so st10 removed one and checked again, and st17 approved. As the bank I acknowledged with one $1,200 item rejected ("account closed"), settled the rest, returned the hospital item the next day, and sent a statement. As recon I took and resolved the exceptions. The maintainers corrected payee a's account and a second maintainer confirmed it, but there was no screen path to send the rejected item again.

Findings:

- **F-15-1 blocker. Nobody can release a held item from the screens.** It hurts the maker, the approver and the payees. Steps: /payments as sub-st10, Act, "Release a held item" (offered to makers). She gets "this needs the approver role" (403). As sub-st17 (the approver) there is no Act form at all, so no Release button. The only way is Controls, then "Another command", typing `{"type":"release_item",...}`. Cause: crates/channels/ui/payments.html:230 sets `can: () => has("maker")`, while crates/payments/src/run.rs:426 needs Approver. Also, when two items are possible duplicates, both twins are held, and releasing one does not release the other.
- **F-15-2 major. A bank-rejected item is not an exception and cannot be sent again.** It hurts the payee and the reconciler. Steps: after the acknowledgement rejected ITM-20261117-A4AQQ, the run shows "rejected: account closed", and Rejected 1200.00 in the totals. The reconciler's "Open reconciliation exceptions" does not list it. After the account was corrected and confirmed, "Send what is unpaid again" on the run is refused with "only an approved run with unpaid items is sent again", although the run is approved and has an unpaid item. The maker has to re-key it into a new run.
- **F-15-3 major. A claim payment the bank returns after settlement never reaches the claim or the waiting list.** It hurts the assessor and the customer. Steps: record_return for PAY-20261117-NDXEV (the SCN-11 hospital payment). The run shows "returned: account closed", but /claims CLM-20261117-UDSC6 still reads "health, paid", Paid 7125.75, Returned empty, and nothing appears in Payments waiting. Cause: crates/payments/src/run.rs:1004-1040 tells claims only when an item failed before settlement (intake.rs:219, failed_before_settlement); a settled return tells only the core (ReturnPayment). (It is possible an outbox relay to claims is not running in the stack; the code path is not there for a settled item.)
- **F-15-4 major. Wrong kind of run, and limits not shown.** A provider (hospital) payment and a $499,883.13 estate payment went into a lump-sum claims run without a word; run types are not matched to what the payment is. The approval refusal "held items are released or removed before approval" does not say which items or who can release them. "Approve" is still offered on an approved run, and st17b's press says "a run is approved after it has been checked", which is misleading.
- **F-15-5 minor. Dates come from the browser clock, not the business date.** The new run's value date defaults to 2026-11-17 against a business date of 2027-11-15 (I changed it by hand). References are CLM-20261117-…/RUN-20261117-…. Reconciliation shows "Days -363/-364". Cause: app.js:341 and 346 (`today = nzDate()`). Partly a test-stack artefact (the business date is a year ahead), but a production day that has not been closed would show the same.
- **F-15-6 minor. Control totals disagree.** "Open runs" shows the run's Total 5500.00 (settled only) while the run held $13,825.75. The run view shows Unsent, At the bank, Rejected, Awaiting, Settled, Returned, but no "submitted = acknowledged + rejected" line and no run total. The reconciliation still shows RUN-20261117-N3YSU-1 −12625.75 "still to show on a statement" after the statement. My statement lines used the wrong references, so that part is my play, not a product finding. An exception can be resolved with free text by one person, and the exception "amount" for a mismatch is the difference, not labelled as such.
- **F-15-7 minor. References are still typed** (compare the "Still typed" table in docs/staff-ui-review.md). Register payee "Party (customer reference)" is typed, even for a hospital that is not in the customer master. Add item "Payee or authority" is typed, as are "Item" for release and remove, a payee in "A payee", and the party in "A customer or payee party". There is no list of payees waiting for confirmation, so payee2 had to know the party. The payee header reads "scn15-party-a-cumy, customer: ." (empty status). "Confirm the details" is offered to the maintainer who entered them (refused: "the person who entered the details cannot confirm them") and stays offered after confirmation. The maintainer picks the "Name check" result herself.
- **F-15-8 minor. "Send what is unpaid again" is offered on a run that has just been prepared.**
- Good: run types read as words; amounts read as dollars (without the $ sign); holds read in words ("possible duplicate", "payee not confirmed", "payee cooling off"); the maker is not offered Approve; a conflicted approver is refused with the reason; settled and rejected show on the run; an item taken out returns to waiting.
