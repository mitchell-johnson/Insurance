Stack notes for the lead (e2e/ not edited):
- Disclosures under v2 (from 2027-11-15) must answer three new required questions: `high_blood_pressure_or_cholesterol`, `asthma_or_lung_condition`, `current_medication`. `anything_else` (v2) is optional free text.
- `quote` now takes the walk-in form `{product_code, frequency, life:{date_of_birth, sex, smoker}, covers:[{code, sum_insured_minor}]}`. The full IssuePolicy body still works. The answer no longer has `partition`; read `total_minor`/`total`.
- `declare_conflict` needs `reason`. `request_evidence` takes an optional `due_on`.
- Customer offers: `GET .../offers/{offer}` gives the terms. A second "look again" is refused.
