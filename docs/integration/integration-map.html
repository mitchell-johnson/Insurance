Integrating the insurance core: modules and what can be swapped

Read from the code and docs on main, 14 Nov 2026. Colour says how replaceable a part is. Dashed means designed but not built. Nothing has been deployed to Azure yet.

Sign-in: Entra ID KEEP

⇅HTTPS with Entra tokens; the portal maps the person to a party ID and checks what they may do (Cedar, signed device approvals)
Front doors and services (stateless, on AKS)

Portal gateway ic-portal SWAP SCREENS

All web pages and app APIs. Replace the pages freely; the consoles carry approvals other modules rely on.

Core API ic-api KEEP

POST /v1/commands, policies, quotes, payments, claims views. The door for your own systems: an Entra app role = the caller name.

Module drivers

Stateless processes that talk to the outside for a module: payments pump, underwriting, reinsurance, unit pricing, documents, group, finance close.

⇅Every system below names a person only by party ID (pty_…) and asks the people master for anything personal
People master: separate from the core, on its own (customer master, ic-customers)

People master SEPARATE · SWAP: FIRST CANDIDATE

Who every person and organisation is: names, contact points, relationships, vulnerability, representatives, due diligence, the customer's own spending hold, retention and erasure. Own PostgreSQL with per-person encryption, not on the ledger, because personal data must be erasable and the ledger never forgets. Your existing CRM or party master can take this role.

Others ask it

POST /v1/parties/match (group) · contact-points (documents) · spending-hold (payments) · routing, authority (core API, before value leaves a policy) · due-diligence (new business, servicing)

It tells others

Deaths to every partition (claims RecordDeath) · owners, assignees, dependants to the portal's directory · holds to the core · events on customer-events

↓Commands: named caller + fixed idempotency key↑Events: gapless, per partition, no personal data⇄Each workload has its own cluster token and grant
Ledger cluster: ic-node × 3, one per availability zone, New Zealand North. Each module runs as its own Raft group on these nodes.

Core: policy engine and ledger KEEP

64 partitions, double-entry hash-chained ledger, products and rates as data, billing, arrears, investment ledger. The only writer of policies and money.

Claims engine KEEP

Lives inside each partition so a decision and its postings commit together. Your claims workbench can sit in front as caller claims.

Daily close ic-worker KEEP

On Temporal (Cloud or self-hosted: undecided). Billing, lapses, earning, reconciliation, signed Merkle root.

Core interfacecommands in: allowed_callers() per commandevents out: EventRecord {partition, seq} and Event Hubs policy-eventspeople only as pty_… IDs

Channels module SWAP SCREENS

Directory of who relates to which policy, devices, staff authorisation, sync feeds.

Payments and collections SEAM

Point at your bank gateway (ISO 20022). Sends SettlePayable, ApplyReceipt; dunning, arrangements.

Finance ledger SEAM

Keep as subledger, feed your ERP. Month-end close, journals, returns, data mart. ERP export not built.

Documents SEAM

Your correspondence platform must send back RecordNoticeDelivery; lapses wait on it.

Complaints and conduct SEAM

Nothing in the core waits on it. Feeds finance conduct facts.

Unit pricing SEAM

A pricing adapter sends ActivateUnitPrice to every partition.

Wellness (AIA Vitality) SEAM

Discount per member. Takes Join, ReportStatus, Leave from caller vitality.

Underwriting HEAVY

Issues policies; reserves reinsurance capacity; group excess; reinstatements; advises commission.

Reinsurance HEAVY

Per-life accumulation, cessions, statements. Underwriting asks it for capacity live.

Adviser commission HEAVY

Posts only via PostCounterparty; workbench, licence cases, finance checks depend on it.

Group schemes HEAVY

Sends group-admin commands; drives matching, underwriting, reinsurance, letters.

Read models KEEP

Policy directory, life index, billing view, inside replicated state.
