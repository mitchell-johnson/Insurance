# Underwriting and new business

`crates/underwriting` (`ic-underwriting`) takes a proposal from quote to policy: the disclosure, the rules, the people who decide what the rules cannot, the offer, and the one command that issues the policy in the core. It is a deterministic state machine beside the core, built to the contract in `docs/modules.md`. Its shipped configuration is in `products/underwriting/` and its authority policy in `config/cedar/underwriting.cedar`.

## What it does

- **Configuration as code, activated through the log** (`config.rs`, `shipped.rs`). Exclusion wordings, reason codes, question sets, rule sets, authority profiles and settings are Starlark files loaded into typed values. Each is activated by a command that names an author, two other approvers and a change reference. Versions only go up, a code list never loses a code, a changed rule set needs its backtest digest, and nobody approves their own profile. Everything is looked up by date, so a proposal is judged by what was in force when it was signed.
- **Two disclosure regimes.** Question set 1 is the duty of disclosure; set 2 is the reasonable-care duty from 15 November 2027. The regime a life answered under is recorded on the file and travels with every later non-disclosure review.
- **No clinical content in the log.** Answers are sealed in the clinical store (`clinical.rs`): AES-256-GCM under a key per person, that key wrapped by a key that never leaves Azure Key Vault, the record's reference bound into the seal, every read given a purpose and recorded. Destroying a person's key ends access to all their records, in backups too, and an erasure marker stops anything more being stored about them. Commands carry hashes, vault references and the per-cover assessment that `prepare_disclosure` works out from the answers before the command is proposed.
- **Signed steps** (`signing.rs`). Disclosure, a tele-interview confirmation, an offer response, a consent and a re-quote are signed on the applicant's device with ed25519 over a message that names the step, so a signature for one step cannot be replayed as another.
- **Routing** (`flow.rs`). The worst disposition across a life's covers decides the path: decline or defer goes to a person for review, a referral opens a case in the right queue, a tele-interview is scheduled, evidence is requested only with the applicant's consent to obtain records, and clean or rule-rated answers are decided by the rules unless the sum insured is over the straight-through limit.
- **Capacity before terms.** Every accepted cover reserves capacity with reinsurance before a decision takes effect. An automatic answer lets it proceed, a facultative answer waits for the reinsurer's terms and applies them, and an excluded answer returns the case to a person. A reservation is released whenever the cover it was for is no longer wanted and confirmed when the policy is issued.
- **Case work under authority** (`casework.rs`, `authority.rs`). Underwriters take, hand over and move cases, request evidence, ask a medical officer's opinion, and propose terms. A Cedar policy and the underwriter's profile decide what each may do: sum insured, loading and exclusion limits by grade, no work on a file where they declared a conflict, no countersigning their own decision. A decline, a deferral, a sum over the countersign limit or a loading over 100% needs a second signature from someone senior who has not touched the file.
- **Offers** (`intake.rs`). Standard terms are accepted at once. Anything else is an offer with an expiry that the applicant accepts, declines or asks to have looked at again. When one of two joint lives drops out, the other is asked again before a single-life policy is issued, because their earlier acceptance was of a different contract.
- **Issue.** When every life is final the module asks the core to issue the policy with one command bound to the quoted product version. On confirmation it confirms the capacity, sends the rating factors to reinsurance and ends any interim cover. A refusal from the core is kept with its reason and can be retried.
- **Interim accidental death cover**, where configured, is requested when the proposal opens, capped, and ended when the proposal ends in any way.
- **The clock** (`external.rs`). One `Sweep` command per day does bounded work in ID order: lapses proposals past their expiry, chases evidence and marks it unobtainable after the configured chasers, escalates cases nobody has touched, and expires offers.
- **Existing cover from the life index (addendum entry 2).** With the applicant's consent, the existing-cover question is pre-filled from the life index across partitions. Without consent nothing is read. What was read, and that it was pre-filled, is recorded on the file, and the applicant's own answer is still theirs to give.
- **A scheme member's consent to itemising.** On a proposal for scheme excess cover the member may agree, by a signed step, that the employer sees a loaded premium (`ConsentKind::EmployerSeesLoading`). The accepted terms carry it to group administration as `employer_may_see_loading`; without it the answer never claims consent.
- **Adviser licence loss (addendum entry 4).** When an adviser's licence is suspended or lost, their access to the file ends at once and the applicant is told. The proposal continues without them.
- **Delegated underwriting** (`special.rs`). A delegate's decisions come in with the delegate's own reference, are checked against the delegated authority (products, sums, loadings, dates, tolerance for late reporting) and are refused or sent to a person where outside it. The delegate's file can be called for and its arrival recorded.
- **Migrated policies.** Terms on a migrated policy are carried as data from the old system. Nothing is defaulted.
- **After issue.** Claims asks for the terms on a cover, the underwriters who touched a life, and the file to be opened for a claim. A non-disclosure review needs seniors who never touched the file and two of them for a remedy. Peer review draws a sample of decisions for a senior who had no part in them.
- **Refusals leave no trace.** A refused command changes nothing. A repeated command gets its first result, marked as a repeat.

## How it talks to the rest

In: commands in an `Envelope` (`key`, `caller`, `at`, `today`, `command`). Callers are fixed names: `config`, `new-business`, `migration`, `adviser-portal`, `evidence`, `underwriting-console`, `reinsurance`, `issuer`, `timer`, `adviser-licensing`, `claims` and `delegate-api`.

Out, on each applied command: events for the audit trail, and `Effect`s, each with a stable key that is its idempotency key at the receiver. Effects are for reinsurance (reserve, release and confirm capacity, record risk factors), the core (issue the policy, issue and end interim cover, apply terms, a non-disclosure remedy), evidence providers (request, chase, cancel), a delegate (send the file) and people (notices).

The reply to a command carries its effects, and the same effects wait, numbered, in a replicated outbox until the driver acknowledges them (`Query::Outbox`, `Command::AcknowledgeEffects` from caller `effects-driver`), so a lost reply loses nothing. An effect that cannot be delivered is parked with its reason (`Command::ParkEffect`, `Query::Parked`) and a person with an authority profile puts it back (`Command::RequeueEffect` from `underwriting-console`).

Queries (`special::Query`, answered by `special::answer`): a proposal, the terms on a policy's cover, the underwriters of a life, a queue with ages, an underwriter's workload, a report over a period, the exclusion library on a date, and the outbox.

For claims: `codes::ExclusionLibrary::matches` decides whether an exclusion applies to a coded cause, and `codes::CAUSE_ROOTS` lists the roots cause codes hang from. Claims owns the coding of a cause; this module owns the wordings and the match. Claims lives inside the core and cannot depend on this crate, so `ExclusionLibrary::for_claims()` produces the same library in the form the claims rules are activated with, and a test holds the two matchers to the same answer for every shipped code.

Hosting: `ic_underwriting::module_spec()` is in `ic_node::modules()`, so every ledger node hosts the module under the name `underwriting`. It starts empty and takes its configuration through the log.

## The driver

`ic-underwriting` (`driver.rs`, `service.rs`, `deploy/k8s/underwriting.yaml`) is the process that carries the outbox. It holds no state and two run at once: every delivery has a key taken from the effect, so each receiver applies a repeat once.

| Effect | Goes to | Answer brought back |
|---|---|---|
| Reserve capacity | Reinsurance module, key `uw:{reservation}:reserve`, then its `capacity` question | `CapacityAnswered`, key `cap:{reservation}` |
| Release and confirm capacity, rating factors | Reinsurance module | none |
| Issue the policy | Core, `IssueQuoted` from `new-business`, which refuses with `product_changed` when the product version is not the one quoted | `PolicyIssued` with the layers the core wrote, or `IssueRejected` |
| Terms for an increase on a policy in force | Core, `AddUnderwrittenLayer` from `underwriting`, one a cover, key `{proposal}/increase/{life}/{cover}`: a new layer from the business date, with the days left of a billed period billed as an adjustment | `PolicyIssued` with the layers, or `IssueRejected` |
| Issue and end interim cover | Core, `IssuePolicy` and `CancelPolicy` | `InterimIssued` |
| Terms or an ended proposal for a group excess case | Group module, `record_excess_decision`, key `uw:{effect key}` | none |
| Notices, evidence requests, delegate file requests | nothing yet | parked with the reason |
| Terms for an alteration on a policy in force | Core, `AlterUnderwrittenTerms` from `underwriting`, one a cover, key `{proposal}/alteration/{life}/{cover}`: each layer of the life's cover on other terms ends and is written again from the business date with the same sum insured, origin and rating date; the days left of a billed period are billed or credited as an adjustment. A cover already on the terms (`no_change`) is nothing to write | `PolicyIssued` with the new layers, or `IssueRejected` |
| Terms for a reinstatement | Core, `ApproveReinstatement` from `underwriting`, key `{proposal}/reinstatement`: the decision and any terms that differ are recorded against the lapse, good until the proposal expires. `Reinstate` (collections or policy servicing, once the arrears are paid) needs it when the lapse has run past the product's `reinstatement_evidence_free_days`, and puts the cover on the terms from that day | `PolicyIssued` with no layers, or `IssueRejected` |
| A non-disclosure remedy | Core, `AvoidPolicy` or `VaryForNonDisclosure` from `underwriting`, key `{review}/remedy`, the review as the case; the core does nothing twice for a case | delivered, or parked with the core's refusal |

A receiver's refusal that means "already done" or "no longer wanted" is counted as stale and acknowledged. Any other refusal parks the effect. Only a receiver that cannot be reached stops a pass, and the next pass starts from the same effect. Group administration's own driver reads the same outbox without acknowledging and sends the same excess decisions under the same key, so either may arrive first.

Once a day the driver sends `Sweep` as caller `timer`, a page at a time, under keys of the day and the page start.

Settings: `IC_NODES` (required), the cluster token as for every worker, `IC_UNDERWRITING_CYCLE_MS` (2000), `IC_UNDERWRITING_PATIENCE_MS` (10000), `IC_UNDERWRITING_BATCH` (100), `IC_UNDERWRITING_SWEEP_PAGE` (200).

## Known gaps

- **Notices, evidence and delegate files have no receiver.** The driver parks them with the reason. Correspondence and the evidence providers need a door for them, and then a `Forward` in the driver.
- **The clinical store has no process and has not been deployed.** The store runs on `ic_azure::keywrap::KeyVaultWrapper` and `ic_azure::records::BlobRecords`, tested against the local stand-in for the Azure endpoints. `infra/underwriting.bicep` creates the wrapping key, a storage account with every kind of delete retention off, and an identity that may only wrap, unwrap and use that container. The template is compiled in CI and has not been deployed, and no process is built yet that takes a disclosure and writes it to the store.
- **Non-disclosure remedies are carried to the core** (`AvoidPolicy`, `VaryForNonDisclosure`, caller `underwriting`, key `{review}/remedy`, the review as the case). A variation carries the terms the cover is treated as having been written on; an avoidance carries whether the premiums paid are refunded or kept, which the two seniors record with the remedy. Which of the two the law allows for a contract needs legal review; the module does not choose. A refund lands in policy suspense and is not paid out automatically. Any refusal (for example `claim_in_payment`) is parked for a person.
- **Evidence-free reinstatement days** (decided by this thread at Mitchell's request, 2026-11-12). Term life: 60 days of its 180-day window. The policy lapses only after 90 days unpaid, so by then the insurer has been unpaid for five months, long enough for a change in health to matter. Health plan: 30 days of its 90-day window, since it lapses sooner (60 days unpaid) and claims follow health changes quickly. Group life: not set. A scheme member's cover comes back through the membership file, and only cover above the free cover limit is underwritten, as excess. Past the evidence-free days a reinstatement needs a reinstatement proposal here; a clean declaration of health goes straight through the rules, anything else goes to an underwriter. The products were changed in place because no version has been in production; once one has, a change like this is a new product version with its own `effective_from`. Nothing yet opens a reinstatement proposal by itself when collections is refused `underwriting_required`: whoever handles the request opens it.
- **A reinstatement reserves no reinsurance capacity**, on the footing that cover inside its reinstatement window is still ceded.
- **An alteration changes underwriting terms only**: loadings and exclusions. It reserves no reinsurance capacity, since the cover is already ceded and its sum insured does not change; reinsurance follows the ended and new layers from the core's events. A change to a rating attribute held on the life (smoker status, occupation class) or to the sum insured is not part of it. A loading added on an alteration is not referred to the reinsurer again, which a facultative placement may require.
- **A group excess proposal is marked issued by group administration**, which sends `policy_issued` (caller `issuer`, key `{request}/excess-issued`) with the layer the core wrote, so the reservation is confirmed against it. `crates/group/tests/underwriting.rs` drives a member's excess through this module's real state machine and includes `tests/common/mod.rs` by path: renaming `World` or its helpers (`new`, `send_keyed`, `disclose`, `automatic`, `days`, `ok`, `clean`, `now`, `today`, `s.outbox`) breaks that test.
- **An increase or an alteration refused for `billing_behind`** (the day's billing has not run) comes back as a failed issue and waits for a person to retry it.
- **No interim product is shipped.** Interim cover is off until settings name a product and cover the core has.
- **Reserve capacity does not carry risk codes.** Reinsurance accepts optional `risk_codes` on a reservation; the effect does not send them, so a treaty exclusion by risk is found at the facultative step and not before.
- **The idempotency key set grows without bound.** The last 20,000 results are kept to answer repeats in full; older keys are remembered but never dropped. It needs a horizon agreed with the drivers.
- **Peer review draws by scanning every proposal**, which is fine for the book the tests build and too slow for a large one.
- **Staff grants are not modelled.** Whether a decision on a staff member's own policy needs a different approver is a channel rule today and is not checked here.
- **No Temporal workflow per proposal.** The design describes one; the state machine, the driver and the daily sweep do the same work with one fewer moving part. Timers are therefore accurate to the day, not the hour.
- **Proposal progress is not published to the portals.** The channels module can take rows; nothing sends them yet.
