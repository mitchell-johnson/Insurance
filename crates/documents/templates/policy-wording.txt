Policy wording

This is the wording of your policy. It says what we cover, what we do not cover, and what you and we must each do. Your policy schedule says which covers you have, the amounts and the dates. Read the two together.

Each cover pays as its section of this wording says. A cover ends on the date your schedule shows, or sooner if the policy ends.

You must pay each premium by its due date. If a premium is not paid, we write to you before your cover ends.

You must tell us the truth when you apply and when you claim. If you do not, we may not pay a claim, and we may end the policy.

You may cancel at any time. Tell us, and the policy ends on the date you choose.
