Insurance portals

Where to go

  • Adviser portal: your book, your standing, and anything held from you.
  • Employer portal: your scheme, its members and its administrators.
  • Staff console: policies, customer requests, your access and the held balance workbench.
  • Complaints and conduct workbench: the register, timers, referrals and conduct cases.
  • Claims console: work queues, a claim, and acting on it as yourself.
  • Finance console: the month-end close, checks, journals and sign-off.
  • Unit pricing console: valuations, price checks, approval, publication and price errors.
  • Reinsurance exceptions: cessions, notices and settlements that need a person.
  • Replacement matches: cover rewritten soon after cover was given up, and disputed matches to decide.
  • Commission console: advisers, engagements, payees, rules, statements and cases in the commission module.
  • Operations console: regions and leaders.
  • Service status: whether everything is working, in plain words.

Customers use the mobile app, which talks to the same service.
