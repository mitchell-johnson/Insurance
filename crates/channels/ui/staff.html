Make a request for the customer

The customer approves it in their app. You cannot approve it for them. A change above your limit, or dated in the past, goes to a second member of staff first.

Changes waiting for a second person

A change a colleague entered for a customer that is above their limit, or dated in the past, needs someone else to agree it before it goes to the customer. Oldest first.

Customers' changes waiting for a trained person

The customer's record says their requests go to a person first. Speak with them, then carry the change out or decline it in words they will read. Why they need a person is not shown here.
