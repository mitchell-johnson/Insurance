Your adviser

Your adviser gives you both references. Appointing them lets them see this policy and prepare changes for you to approve.

Tell us about a claim

Only the cover, what happened and the date go from here. The claims team contacts you for the rest, so nothing about your health is typed into this page.

Waiting for you

Changes your adviser or our staff prepared for you. Nothing happens until you approve. Approving is signed, so set up this browser under Signing first, or approve on your phone.

Your applications

Cover you have applied for. When we offer terms, the offer letter is in your documents; accept it, decline it or ask us to look again here. If the product changes before your cover starts, the new price is shown here for you to accept. Your answer is signed.

Your requests

Cover from your workplace

If your employer's scheme covers you, link it to you with the scheme number and employee ID from your employer. When you leave the scheme you may keep your cover as a policy of your own, with no health questions, until the date shown.

Your details

Hold on money going out

With a hold on, nothing is paid out of your policies, such as a withdrawal or a surrender, until you lift it. Lifting it takes a week, so anyone pressing you has to wait.

A copy of everything we hold

Under the Privacy Act you can have a copy of everything we hold about you. It comes as a file.

Signing on this browser

Approving a change that moves money, changing your details and taking a copy of your record need a signature from a device you have set up. The key is made in this browser and never leaves it.
